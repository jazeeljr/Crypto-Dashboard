Cryptocurrency Market Analysis Dashboard
Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze historical cryptocurrency market data from 2015 to 2021. The dashboard provides insights into market capitalization, trading volume, and price movements across multiple cryptocurrencies.

Features
Interactive filters for selecting cryptocurrencies and years
KPI cards displaying Market Capitalization, Trading Volume, High, Low, and Average prices
Market Capitalization trend analysis over time
Comparison of Open vs Close prices
Comparison of High vs Low prices
Multi-page dashboard for exploring different aspects of cryptocurrency performance
Dataset

The dashboard uses historical market data of eight cryptocurrencies, including:

Bitcoin
Ethereum
Dogecoin
Binance Coin
Cardano
Tether
XRP
USD Coin
Tools Used
Power BI Desktop
DAX
Microsoft Excel (Data Preparation)
Dashboard Pages
Page 1 – Market Overview

Provides an overview of cryptocurrency performance through KPI cards and Market Capitalization trends.

Page 2 – Price Analysis

Focuses on price fluctuations using High/Low and Open/Close comparisons to better understand market volatility.

Key Insights
Bitcoin maintained the highest market capitalization among the selected cryptocurrencies.
Significant fluctuations in trading volume and prices were observed during different periods.
Interactive filters allow users to compare trends across coins and years efficiently.
